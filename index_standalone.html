CHEMISTRY

Redox Reactions Simplified

Oxidation is Loss of electrons (or addition of Oxygen/removal of Hydrogen). Reduction is Gain of electrons. The substance getting oxidised is the reducing agent!

BIOLOGY

Double Circulation in Human Heart

Blood goes through the heart twice in one complete cardiac cycle: Pulmonary Circulation (Heart → Lungs → Heart) and Systemic Circulation (Heart → Body Organs → Heart).

COMMON PITFALL #1

Sign Convention in Optics

Object distance u is always negative in Cartesian sign convention. Many students forget this and get reversed focal length values!

COMMON PITFALL #2

Linear Equations: Consistency Check

Parallel lines (a1/a2 = b1/b2 ≠ c1/c2) have NO solution and are Inconsistent. Coincident lines are Dependent Consistent.
